Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026  ·  Last Updated: March 23, 2026
Summary: VietLong Creator does not collect or store your personal data on any server. All data stays in your browser. Your TikTok credentials are never logged or shared.

1. Who We Are

VietLong Creator is operated by Lê Trần Việt Long, accessible at letranvietlong.github.io/VietLongCreator.html. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information only when you connect your TikTok account:

  • TikTok Display Name — shown in the app UI only
  • TikTok Avatar URL — displayed in the app UI only
  • Follower Count — displayed in the app UI only
  • Access Token & Refresh Token — stored only in your browser's localStorage
  • Open ID — used to authenticate uploads, stored in localStorage

We do not collect: email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or browsing history.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your TikTok profile within the app
  • To authenticate video uploads to your TikTok account when you explicitly request it
  • To maintain your login session between visits (via localStorage)

4. Data Storage

All user data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage:

  • No data is sent to or stored on VietLong Creator servers
  • Our Vercel proxy temporarily processes OAuth tokens during login — these are not logged or stored
  • You can clear all data by logging out or clearing your browser's localStorage

5. TikTok API Data

VietLong Creator uses TikTok's official APIs under TikTok's Terms of Service. Data received from TikTok APIs is used solely within the app to provide core functionality. We comply with TikTok's Platform Policy regarding data handling and retention.

TikTok's Privacy Policy: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. The only external service involved is:

  • TikTok API — for authentication and video publishing (at your request)
  • Vercel — as an OAuth proxy (processes but does not store token data)

7. Data Retention

Since all data is stored in your browser's localStorage, you control retention entirely. Clearing localStorage or logging out immediately removes all stored data. We retain no copies on any server.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view it directly in your browser's localStorage
  • Delete your data — log out or clear localStorage at any time
  • Revoke TikTok access — disconnect in TikTok's app settings under "Manage app permissions"

9. Children's Privacy

VietLong Creator is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the Service, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will indicate the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns: letranvietlong@gmail.com
